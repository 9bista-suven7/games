# Snake

Snake, the old Nokia phone classic, in a single HTML file: a green LCD screen in a wooden frame, with smooth movement, a snake with eyes and a flicking tongue, and a colourful Modern look if you prefer.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed. Your best scores and settings are remembered in your browser.

## Rules

- Steer the snake to the food. Each bite is worth **10** points and makes the snake one longer.
- Don't hit your own body. With **Walls on** the edge is deadly; with **Wrap round** you come out on the other side.
- Every 5th bite a **bonus** appears for a short time. It blinks before it disappears and is worth **50**.
- The snake gets a little faster every 4 bites.
- **Maze** mode adds rocks in one of four layouts (Pillars, Bars, Box, Corners), picked at random each game.
- The best score is kept separately for each speed (Slow, Normal, Fast), walls setting and mode.

Controls: **swipe** on the board, the on-screen **D-pad**, or the **arrow keys** / **W A S D**. You can line up two quick turns for a tight U-turn. **Space** pauses, **Esc** opens the menu.
