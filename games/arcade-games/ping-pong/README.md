# Ping Pong

Ping Pong (table tennis) in a single HTML file: a top-down table with a real bouncing ball, against the computer or a friend on the same device.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed. Your wins against the computer are remembered in your browser.

## Controls

- **Drag** anywhere to move your bat; it follows your finger with a little lag. With a mouse, the bat follows the pointer.
- Where the ball meets the bat sets the direction (left edge, middle, right edge). **Swipe towards the net** as you hit for pace and topspin; a fast swipe is a **smash**. Swiping sideways adds sidespin, swiping back gives a soft chop.
- **Serve**: swipe forward or tap.
- Keys: **arrows** or **WASD** to move (hold **Shift** for more speed and power), **Space** to serve, **Esc** for the menu.
- Upright phones show the table vertically (you at the bottom); sideways phones and desktops show it horizontally (you on the left).

## Rules

- The serve must bounce once on your side, then once on the other side. A serve that clips the net and lands correctly is a **let**: serve again.
- After that, every shot must bounce **once** on the other side before it is hit back.
- You lose the point if you miss, let the ball bounce twice, hit it into the net, off the table, or onto your own side.
- The serve changes every 2 points, and every point from 10–10.
- First to **11** wins the game, but you must win by 2 (**deuce** from 10–10). Play 1 game, or best of 3 or 5.
- **vs Computer**: Easy, Medium or Hard. Harder computers react faster, move quicker, aim closer to the lines and smash more.
- **2 players**: one device, one end each. Both players drag at once on their own half of the screen. On a keyboard, WASD and Space for the first player, arrows and Enter for the second.
