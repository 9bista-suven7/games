# Changa Chet

Changa Chet (चङ्गा चेट) in a single HTML file: Dashain kite fighting over the Kathmandu rooftops. Cross strings with the rival kites, cut theirs and keep yours. Flying kites is one of the best-loved Dashain traditions: in the weeks of the festival the sky over the valley fills with kites, and every rooftop shouts "Changa chet!" when a string is cut.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed. Your best Dashain Sky score is remembered in your browser.

## Rules

- **Hold** (on the sky, the **Pull** button, or Space) to pull: your kite flies the way its nose points, and you wind in a little string.
- **Let go** to give string (**dheel**): the kite drifts with the wind, sinks slowly and its nose turns. Pull again when it points where you want to go.
- The **manjha** meter is the string left on your reel. More string lets you fly further and higher; when the reel is empty the kite just hangs on the wind.
- **Simple controls** (menu): hold to climb, drag left or right to steer (or ← →).
- **Pecha**: when two strings cross they lock. Quick, steady pulls, about three a second (**tanne**), or a long steady **dheel** (only while there is string on your reel) push the tug-of-war meter. Holding a pull barely helps. Reach the end and the other string snaps; after 6 seconds whoever is behind loses. Good rhythm beats dheel, dheel beats sloppy pulling.
- **Loot**: touch a falling cut kite with your string for +50.
- Points: +100 per kite cut, +50 for each extra cut within 4 seconds (Double chet!), +50 per loot, +2 a second in the high sky.
- **Dashain Sky**: endless. Cut kites are flown again and the rivals get sharper. It ends when your kite is cut.
- **Battle**: you and 3 rivals (hunter, cautious, wanderer), 3 rounds, no second kites. The last kite flying wins the round (+200). Most rounds won takes the battle, points break ties. Rounds last up to 2 minutes.

Keys: **Space** or **↑** to pull, **←/→** to steer with simple controls, **Esc** for the menu.
