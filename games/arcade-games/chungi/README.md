# Chungi

Chungi (चुङ्गी) in a single HTML file: keep a bundle of rubber bands in the air with your feet while your friends count out loud. A classic Nepali street game, played in every galli and school ground.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed. Your best solo count is remembered in your browser.

## Rules

- **Start**: your first tap tosses the chungi up, so take your time.
- **Kick**: tap the galli, the **Kick** button or **Space** as the chungi drops to your foot. The ring round it closes at the best height. You get **Perfect!**, **Good**, or a poor kick (**Early**, **Late** or **Wide**). Poor kicks go lower and fly off sideways.
- **Move**: drag left and right, hold **◀ ▶**, or use **← →** (A, D) to get under the chungi as it drifts. Taking it a little behind your foot pops it back; a little ahead sends it forward.
- If it touches the ground, your turn is over. Every kick counts, and the count is your score: एक, दुई, तीन…
- **Tricks**: swipe up for **Knee** (W, ↑), swipe left for **Inside** foot (Q), swipe right for **Outside** foot (E), double-tap for **Heel** with the chungi behind you. Each has its own timing and arc. Bonus points: Knee 2, Inside and Outside 3, Heel 5, doubled for a Perfect, plus 1 for any Perfect.
- **Chain**: different tricks one after another build a multiplier up to ×5. The same trick twice starts a new chain; a plain kick or a poor kick ends it.
- The timing gets tighter as your count goes up, and from 15 kicks the wind starts to gust.
- **Solo**: beat your best count. **Friends & bots**: 2 to 4 players (any of them bots, Easy, Medium or Hard) take turns on one device, 1 to 3 turns each. The most kicks wins; trick points break a tie.

Keys: **Space** to kick (twice for a heel), **W/↑**, **Q**, **E** for tricks, **←/→** to move, **Esc** for the menu.
