# Dandi Biyo

Dandi Biyo (डण्डी बियो) in a single HTML file: flick the biyo up, hit it as far as you can, and keep away from the fielders. A traditional Nepali village game, cousin of gilli-danda.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed. Your best solo total is remembered in your browser.

## Rules

- The **biyo** (a short peg, tapered at both ends) lies across the **gucchi** (a small pit). You hit it with the **dandi** (a long stick).
- Each hit takes two taps. **Flick**: tap when the swinging needle is in the green to pop the biyo up; the nearer the middle, the higher it pops. **Hit**: tap again as the ring closes on the biyo in the air. You get **Perfect!**, **Good** or **Weak**.
- Set the **Loft** (lever, drag on the field, or ↑/↓) for a low drive or a high hit. High hits go further, but the fielders have more time to catch them.
- Distance is counted in dandi lengths from the gucchi to where the biyo stops, and adds up over the innings.
- You have **3 lives**. A failed flick, a missed swing or a catch costs one. The fielders move after every hit, and the meter speeds up as the innings goes on.
- **Solo challenge**: one innings, beat your best. **Friends & bots**: 2 to 4 players (any of them bots, Easy, Medium or Hard) bat in turn on one device, 1 to 3 innings each. The highest total wins.

Keys: **Space** or **Enter** to tap, **↑/↓** for loft, **Esc** for the menu.
