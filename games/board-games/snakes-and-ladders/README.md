# Snakes & Ladders

Snakes & Ladders (often called Ludo snake, लुडो स्नेक, in Nepal) in a single HTML file: play against 1 to 3 bots, or pass one device around for 2 to 4 people.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed. Your last mode, player count, names and board options are remembered in your browser.

## Rules

- Everyone starts off the board. Tap the dice to roll and your token walks that many squares, from 1 (bottom left) up the zig-zag rows to 100.
- Roll a **6** and you roll again after your move.
- Stop at the bottom of a **ladder** to climb it. Stop on a **snake's head** to slide down to its tail. Passing over them does nothing.
- **Exact roll to finish** (on by default): you must land exactly on 100. Roll too much and you bounce back the extra squares, and a snake where you stop still bites. Turn it off and reaching or passing 100 wins.
- 15 seconds to roll. When time runs out, the game rolls for you.
- The first player to reach 100 wins. You can play on for 2nd and 3rd place or go straight to the standings.

Boards: the **classic** layout (9 ladders, 10 snakes), or turn on **Random board** for a new layout every game: 6 to 8 ladders and 7 to 9 snakes, no shared squares, nothing on 1 or 100, and no ladder or snake end hidden under another piece.

Modes: **Play vs bots** (you are Red, bots take the other colours) and **Pass & play** (each colour gets a name). With 2 players the colours are Red and Yellow, with 3 players Red, Green and Yellow.
