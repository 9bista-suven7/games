    How to play

    The goal

    • Race your token from the start, up the board from square 1 (bottom left) to square 100 (top). The rows zig-zag: left to right, then right to left.
    • The first player to reach 100 wins. The others can play on for 2nd and 3rd place.

    Your turn

    • Tap the dice (or press Space) to roll, and your token moves that many squares by itself. Everyone starts off the board, so a roll of 4 puts you on square 4.
    • Roll a 6 and you get another roll straight after your move.
    • You have 15 seconds to roll. When time runs out, the game rolls for you.

    Ladders and snakes

    • Stop at the bottom of a ladder and climb straight up to its top.
    • Stop on a snake's head and slide all the way down to its tail.
    • Only where you stop counts: passing over a ladder or a snake does nothing. Any number of tokens can share a square.

    Finishing

    • Exact roll to finish (on): you must land exactly on 100. If you roll too much, you walk up to 100 and bounce back the extra squares, and a snake where you stop still bites.
    • With it off, reaching or passing 100 wins.

    Boards and modes

    • The classic board has 9 ladders and 10 snakes. Turn on Random board on the start screen for a fresh layout every game.
    • Play vs bots: you are Red against 1 to 3 bots. Pass & play: 2 to 4 people share one device, each with their own colour and name.
