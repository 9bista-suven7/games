    How to play

    The goal

    • Each player has 4 tokens in their coloured yard. Move all four once around the board, up your coloured home column and into the home triangle in the centre.
    • The first player with all four tokens home wins. Everyone else plays on for 2nd and 3rd place.

    Your turn

    • Tap the dice to roll. You need a 6 to bring a token out onto your coloured start square.
    • Tokens that can move pulse: tap one to move it forward by the number rolled. Tokens go clockwise round the board. If only one move is possible, it moves by itself.
    • Reaching home needs the exact number. A token 3 squares from home can move with a 1, 2 or 3, but not a 4.
    • You have 20 seconds to roll and 20 to move. When time runs out, the game rolls or moves for you.

    Extra rolls

    • Roll again after a 6, after a capture and after a token reaches home.
    • Three 6s in a row: your turn ends straight away and the third 6 is not played.

    Capturing and safe squares

    • Land exactly on an opponent's token on a plain square to capture it: it goes back to its yard. If several opponent tokens share that square, they all go back.
    • Star squares and the four coloured start squares are safe. Tokens of any colour can share them and nobody is captured there.
    • Tokens in a home column are safe too. There are no blocks: any token can pass any other.

    Modes

    • Play vs bots: you are Red; bots take the other colours (2, 3 or 4 players).
    • Pass & play: 2 to 4 people share one device, each with their own colour and name.
