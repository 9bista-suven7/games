# Ludo

Ludo (लुडो) in a single HTML file: play against 1 to 3 bots, or pass one device around for 2 to 4 people.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed. Your last mode, player count and names are remembered in your browser.

## Rules

- Each player has 4 tokens in their yard. Roll a **6** to bring a token out onto your coloured start square.
- Tap the dice to roll, then tap a pulsing token to move it. If only one move is possible, it moves by itself.
- Tokens go clockwise round the board, then up your coloured home column. Reaching home needs the **exact** number.
- Land on an opponent's token on a plain square to **capture** it and send it back to its yard. If several opponent tokens share that square, they all go back.
- **Star squares** and the four **coloured start squares** are safe: tokens of any colour can share them. Home columns are safe too. There are no blocks.
- Roll again after a **6**, a **capture**, or a token reaching **home**. **Three 6s in a row** ends your turn, and the third 6 is not played.
- 20 seconds to roll and 20 to move. When time runs out, the game rolls or moves for you.
- The first player with all four tokens home wins. The others play on for 2nd and 3rd place, then the final standings are shown.

Modes: **Play vs bots** (you are Red, bots take the other colours) and **Pass & play** (each colour gets a name). With 2 players the colours are Red and Yellow, with 3 players Red, Green and Yellow.
