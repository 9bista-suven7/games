        How to play

        The goal

        • Each side has 9 coins: White or Black. Flick the striker to knock your coins into the four corner pockets.
        • The first side to pocket all 9 wins the board, but only once the red queen has been pocketed and covered (by either side).

        Your shot

        • Slide the striker along your baseline, between the two red circles: drag the striker, or use the slider in the frame below it. It can't sit on top of a coin.
        • Pull back from the striker like a slingshot and let go. The dotted line shows where it goes and the first piece it hits; the ring and the bar show the power.
        • Shots must go forward, away from your own cushion. Coins behind your baseline can only be reached off a cushion.
        • You have 30 seconds. When time runs out, a shot is played for you towards your nearest coin.
        • Aim + Strike (choose it in the menu): tap or drag on the board to aim, set the power bar, then press Strike. Keyboard: ←/→ aim, ↑/↓ power, A/D move the striker, Space strikes.

        Extra shots and the queen

        • Pocket one of your own coins (or the queen) and you shoot again.
        • You may pocket the queen once you have pocketed at least one of your coins (a coin in the same shot counts too).
        • Then cover it: pocket one of your own coins in the same shot or your very next one. If you don't, the queen goes back to the centre.

        Fouls

        • Striker pocketed: your turn ends and one of your pocketed coins goes back to the centre (if you have one). A queen pocketed on that shot goes back too.
        • Opponent coins you pocket count for them. You can't pocket their last coin: it goes back and your turn ends.
        • Your own last coin can't go in before the queen is covered: it goes back and your turn ends.

        Scoring

        • The board winner scores 1 point for every opponent coin still on the board, plus 3 if they covered the queen.
        • Matches: 1 board, 3 boards (most points wins; if level, most boards won) or first to 25 points. White breaks the first board, then the break alternates.

        Modes

        • vs Computer: you play White from the bottom; the computer plays Black from the top. Easy misses often, Medium plays solid cut shots, Hard rarely misses.
        • Pass & play: 2 players (White against Black) or 4 players, where partners sit across from each other and share a colour. Turns go round the table and the board turns so the shooter is always at the bottom.
