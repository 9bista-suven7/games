    How to play

    The deal

    • 4 players, 52 cards, 13 cards each. A hand with no spades is redealt.
    • Spades (♠︎) are always trump. Play passes anticlockwise.

    The call

    • Starting after the dealer, each player calls how many tricks they will win (1–13).

    The play

    • The player after the dealer leads the first trick; the trick winner leads the next.
    • Follow suit, and play a card higher than the one currently winning if you can.
    • Out of that suit? You must play a spade, beating any spade already played if you can.
    • No spades, or none high enough? Play any card.
    • The highest spade wins the trick; with no spades, the highest card of the led suit wins.

    Scoring

    • Make your call: +call, plus 0.1 for each extra trick (call 3, win 5 → 3.2).
    • Miss your call: −call (call 4, win 3 → −4).
    • After 5 rounds, the highest total wins.

    Playing with friends

    • Tap Create table and let friends scan the QR code, or send them the link or table code.
    • Empty seats are played by bots. If someone leaves mid-game, a bot takes over their seat.
    • The host's device runs the game, so the host should keep the page open until the end.
