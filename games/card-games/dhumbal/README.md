# Dhumbal

Nepali Dhumbal (धुम्बल), also called Jhyap, in a single HTML file: you against three bots. Keep your hand as low as you can.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed.

## Rules

- 4 players, one deck, 5 cards each. One card is turned face up to start the open cards; the rest is the draw pile. Play goes clockwise, starting after the dealer.
- Card values: A = 1, 2–10 face value, J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13.
- On your turn, throw one of: a single card, a set of 2–4 cards of the same rank, or a run of 3+ cards in a row in one suit (Ace is low only: A-2-3 is fine, Q-K-A is not).
- Then pick up one card: the top of the draw pile, or any one of the cards the previous player just threw.
- **Jhyap**: if the card you draw from the pile matches the rank of the single card or set you just threw, you may throw it straight away too.
- **Dhumbal**: with a total of 5 or less, call Dhumbal instead of throwing. Everyone shows their hand. If the caller is strictly lowest, the caller scores 0 and everyone else adds their hand total. If anyone ties or is lower, the caller is caught: +25 plus their own total, the lowest player(s) score 0, the rest add their totals.
- Reach 100 penalty points and you're out. The last player left wins.
- You have 25 seconds per turn. When time runs out, your highest card is thrown and you draw from the pile.
