How to play

The goal

  • Keep the cards in your hand as low as possible. A = 1, 2–10 count their number, J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13.
  • 4 players, one deck, 5 cards each. One card is turned face up to start the open cards; the rest is the draw pile.
  • Play goes clockwise. The player after the dealer starts, and the dealer moves on each round.

Your turn

  1. If your total is 5 or less, you may call Dhumbal! instead of playing (see below).
  2. Otherwise throw: tap cards to select them, then tap Throw. You can throw one of these:
  1. Single: any one cardK♣
  2. Set: 2, 3 or 4 cards of the same rank7♠ 7♥
  3. Run: 3 or more in a row, same suit4♥ 5♥ 6♥
  4. Not allowed: mixed suits, or Ace above King (Ace is low only)Q♠ K♠ A♠
  1. Then pick up one card: the top of the draw pile, or any one of the cards the previous player just threw. You can't take back what you just threw.

Jhyap!

  • If the card you draw from the pile has the same rank as the single card or set you just threw, you may throw it straight away too. No extra pickup.
  • Example: you throw 7♣, draw 7♦: tap Jhyap! and both 7s are gone.

Calling Dhumbal

  • Everyone shows their hand. If the caller has the strictly lowest total, the caller scores 0 and everyone else adds their hand total as penalty points.
  • If anyone ties or is lower, the caller is caught: the caller adds 25 + their total, the lowest player(s) score 0, and the others add their totals.
  • Example: you call with 4, Bikash has 3. Caught! You get 29, Bikash 0, the others add their totals.

Winning

  • Penalty points add up over the rounds. Reach 100 or more and you're out. The last player left wins.
  • You have 25 seconds per turn. When time runs out, your highest card is thrown and you draw from the pile.
  • If the draw pile runs out, the older thrown cards are shuffled into a new pile.
