How to play

Kauda (कौडा) is played across Nepal during Tihar, and almost every family and village has its own rules. This is one clear, common-style version: you and three bots bet against the mat (the bank) with free play chips only, no real money.

The toss

  • Each round the tosser shakes 16 cowrie shells and throws them on the mat. The tosser changes every round.
  • The result is how many land open, mouth up: anything from 0 to 16. Every shell is a fair 50/50, so 7, 8 and 9 come up most and 0 or 16 almost never.

Betting

  • Pick a chip (10, 50, 100 or 500) and tap a box on the mat to bet. Tap again to add more. You can bet on as many boxes as you like, up to 5,000 per box.
  • Exact number 1 to 16: wins if exactly that many land open.
  • Low (0 to 6), Middle (7 to 9) and High (10 to 16). An all-closed toss (0) counts only as Low and Even.
  • Odd or Even (0 counts as even).
  • Clear takes back your bets, Repeat places last round's bets again, and Toss closes betting. You have 20 seconds; then the shells are thrown anyway.

Payouts

  • ×N means a winning bet pays back N times the bet, including the bet itself. 100 on Middle at ×1.75 returns 175.
  • Each payout comes from the real chance of that result with a small house edge of about 5%, rounded to a friendly number.

Play chips have no cash value and cannot be bought or cashed out. If you run out, you get a free refill.
