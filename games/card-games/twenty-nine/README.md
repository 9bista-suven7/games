# Twenty-Nine

Twenty-Nine (२९) in a single HTML file: you and a bot partner against two bot opponents in the partnership trick-taking game played all over Nepal.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed.

## Rules

- 32 cards (J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7 of each suit). In each suit they rank **J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7**.
- Card points: **J = 3, 9 = 2, A = 1, 10 = 1**, others 0, so 28 points per deal.
- Everyone gets 4 cards and bids 16–28 or passes; the auction ends when three have passed. If the first three pass, the dealer must bid.
- The highest bidder picks trump by placing a card of that suit face down. Then 4 more cards each.
- Follow suit if you can. If you can't and trump is still hidden, you may tap **Show trump**; you must then play a trump if you have one.
- Before trump is revealed, the highest card of the suit led wins. After, the highest trump wins. A hidden trump is revealed at the start of the last trick.
- **Pair**: after trump is revealed, K and Q of trump in one hand moves the target 4 down (bidder's team) or 4 up (opponents), within 16–28. You need to have won a trick first.
- Bidder's team reaches the target: +1 game point, otherwise −1. First team to +6 wins; a team at −6 loses.

House rules vary a lot (a last-trick point making 29, redeals, doubling). This is a common Nepali/Indian version.
