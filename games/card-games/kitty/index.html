How to play

The round

  • Everyone puts the boot (100) into the pot and gets 9 cards.
  • Split your 9 cards into 3 hands of 3 cards: Hand 1, Hand 2 and Hand 3.
  • Tap a card to put it in the highlighted hand. Tap a card in a hand to take it back, or tap a hand to fill that one next.
  • Auto arrange picks a strong split for you. You have 60 seconds; when time runs out your cards are arranged for you.

Showdown

  • Everyone shows Hand 1 together. The best Hand 1 wins that show. Then Hand 2, then Hand 3.
  • If two or more players tie for the best hand in a show, nobody wins that show.
  • Win 2 or 3 shows to win the round and take the whole pot.
  • If nobody wins 2 shows, it's Kitty: the pot carries over to the next round and keeps growing.
  • Tip: winning two shows is what counts, so two good hands often beat one great hand and two weak ones.
