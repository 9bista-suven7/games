# Kitty

Nepali Kitty (किटी) in a single HTML file: you against three bots, with free play chips only (no real money).

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step needed. Your chip balance is remembered in your browser.

## Rules

- Everyone puts the boot (100) in the pot and gets 9 cards.
- Split your 9 cards into 3 hands of 3 cards: Hand 1, Hand 2 and Hand 3. Tap a card to put it in the highlighted hand; tap it again to take it back. **Auto arrange** picks a strong split. You have 60 seconds.
- Showdown: everyone shows Hand 1 together and the best one wins that show. Then Hand 2, then Hand 3. A tie for the best hand means nobody wins that show.
- Win 2 or 3 shows to win the round and take the whole pot.
- If nobody wins 2 shows, it's **Kitty**: the pot carries over to the next round.

Hand ranking, best first: Trail, Pure Sequence, Sequence, Color, Pair, High Card. A-K-Q is the top sequence, then A-2-3.
