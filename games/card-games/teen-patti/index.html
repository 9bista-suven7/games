How to play

The hand

  • Everyone puts the boot (100) into the pot and gets 3 cards face down.
  • On your turn you can stay Blind (cards unseen) or tap See to look at them.
  • Blind players bet the current stake; seen players bet double. Raise doubles the stake.
  • Pack to fold. The last player left, or the best hand at a show, wins the pot.

Show and sideshow

  • Show: when only two players are left, pay your bet and both hands are compared.
  • Sideshow: a seen player can ask the previous seen player to compare privately. If they accept, the weaker hand packs.
  • On a tie, the player who asked for the show or sideshow loses.
  • When the pot reaches the pot limit, everyone left shows automatically.

Hand ranking (best first)

  1. 1Trail: three of a kindA♠ A♥ A♦
  2. 2Pure sequence: run in one suitQ♥ J♥ 10♥
  3. 3Sequence: run in mixed suits7♣ 6♦ 5♠
  4. 4Color: three of one suitK♣ 9♣ 4♣
  5. 5Pair: two of a kind8♠ 8♥ 3♣
  6. 6High cardA♦ 10♣ 6♠
  • Sequences: A-K-Q is highest, then A-2-3, then K-Q-J and down.
