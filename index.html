Suven's Game Room

Nepali card, board and arcade games that run right in your browser. No install, no sign-up. Open one on your phone and play.

Dhumbal

Throw singles, sets or runs and keep your hand total low. Call Dhumbal at 5 or less, but don't get caught.

Nepali rummy with three decks. Show three pure sequences to see the Maal, then finish with jokers.

The Tihar cowrie game. Bet on how many of the 16 shells land open, then watch them fly.

DashainPlay chips only

Draw blind from your neighbour and throw down pairs of the same colour. Don't get stuck with the odd card.

4 playersEasy for kids

Board games

Ludo

Roll a six to come out, capture your rivals and race all four tokens home. Play bots or pass the phone around.

2–4 playersPass & play

Snakes & Ladders

Climb the ladders, dodge the snakes and land exactly on 100. Classic board or a fresh random one each game.

2–4 playersPass & play

Carrom

Flick the striker with real physics. Pocket your coins, cover the queen, and race to 25.

vs Computer2–4 pass & play

Full rules with castling, en passant and promotion. Play the computer at three levels or a friend on one device.
